About LeBlanc Consulting Ghana

A trusted construction and maintenance partner serving Ghana since 2011.

Our Story

Ghana-Based.
Built to Last.

LeBlanc Consulting Ghana was established to bring reliable, professional construction and maintenance services to a market that deserved better. We started with a small team of skilled artisans and a simple promise: show up on time, do the job right, and stand behind our work.

Today we serve residential homeowners, commercial property managers, and industrial clients across Greater Accra and beyond. Our bilingual team handles everything from small repairs to large construction projects with the same level of care and professionalism.

We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured — and we maintain strict safety and quality standards on every site, every day.
